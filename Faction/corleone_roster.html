Faction Reformat Proposal, Draft 1

The Corleone Family

"Rank Structure & Rationale"

Overview

This proposal splits the family into five tiers: leadership at the top, department heads who each own one function, a single cross-department liaison who organizes between departments and backs up any of them, a broad officer rank, and a five-step prestige ladder for members who haven't been made yet. The prestige ladder has no permission changes, it's there to give rank-and-file members something to climb toward.

Leadership

3 titles
Full or near-full permissions. Runs the family and its direction.
TitleDescription
Leader
Fixed		 The head of the family, carrying every permission with no restrictions. Sets the family's overall direction, has final say on major decisions, and answers for the faction as a whole.
Co-Leader
Fixed		 Second-in-command, one step below the Leader with the same full access. Keeps the family running day-to-day and picks up leadership duties whenever the Leader is unavailable.
Underboss
Senior Leadership		 Historically the Don's most trusted second, running daily operations in his stead. Oversees the department heads as a group, steps into any decision that needs leadership-level judgment, and keeps the family's overall strategy on track.

Department Heads

4 titles
Elevated permissions, scoped to one function of the family's operation.
TitleDescription
Caporegime
Organized Crime		 Italian for "head of the crew," the traditional boss of a Cosa Nostra crew running its rackets. Owns Organized Crime end to end, keeping utilities stocked and payouts moving, tracking who's currently slotted into an OC, and optimizing crews around role weight and members' Crime Skill (CS).
Consigliere
War Strategist		 Italian for "counselor," the Don's traditional advisor on strategy and conflict with outside families. Runs the war effort: negotiating terms, assigning chain watchers and chain bonuses, developing new strategies, and putting out war newsletters.
Contabile
Treasury		 Italian for "the accountant," the one who keeps the books straight. Oversees the armory and the family's funds: managing war caches, tracking who's donated what, reimbursing members from the faction bank, handling war payouts, and weighing in on negotiated war terms.
Sensale
Human Resources		 Italian for "matchmaker" or "broker," historically the member who vouches for and introduces a prospective recruit into the family. Runs recruitment and onboarding as the family's HR arm: advertising the faction, sourcing new members, walking them through onboarding and TornStats setup, and carrying heavy say over promotions, accolades, and profile badges.

Cross-Department Liaison

1 title
A single seat. Organizes between departments and can back up any department head; permissions are spread thin across all fields rather than deep in one.
TitleDescription
Luogotenente
Lieutenant		 Italian for "lieutenant," literally "one who holds the place," a proxy who acts with a superior's authority in their absence. An all-around trusted member who helps out each department head without owning one of their own: can fulfill the tasks of any department, though limited in decision-making and management, and also helps organize events and giveaways.

Officer

1 title
Broader permissions than rank-and-file, below department heads and the Luogotenente. Made Men can also step in as informal backup.
TitleDescription
Made Man
Officer		 The real meaning of "made" is someone who's earned enough trust to operate with real standing. Fits broader access than rank-and-file without full leadership authority.

Prestige Ladder

5 titles · no permission change
Purely status and motivation, the climb toward being made.
Leva→ Cugine→ Soldato→ Sgarrista→ Capodecina
TitleDescription
Leva
Entry		 Trial/recruit stage; mirrors the current probation period, signals "still proving yourself."
Cugine
Prestige		 Slang for an up-and-comer actively trying to get made. Built-in motivation to stay active.
Soldato
Prestige		 A proven, dependable soldier. First real mark of respect above trial status.
Sgarrista
Prestige		 Trusted senior soldier with real standing, but not yet leading anything. The "you've clearly earned this" tier.
Capodecina
Prestige		 Informally leads and mentors without official authority. Last rung before Made Man, so top members have a visible "you're next" target.
