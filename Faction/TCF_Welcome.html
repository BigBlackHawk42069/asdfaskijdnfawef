Article IWelcome to the Family

We are the Corleone Family — bound by loyalty, tested by war, and strengthened by every hand that joins us.

To carry our name is to fight together, earn together, and grow together. Whether you come to us seasoned or unproven, you will never stand alone; every member of this House stands behind you.

Article IIIThe Call to War

"Ranked War is the Family's true purpose."
  1. Stack before every war is called.
  2. Follow those who call the chain.
  3. Remain in the fight through every push.
  4. Silence loses wars — speak up.
  5. When uncertain, ask; never guess.

Article IVWhat the Family Provides

In return for your loyalty, the Family provides for its own: payouts worthy of the war fought, leadership that has bled for this name, and counsel on every stat that matters. Every crime is run in concert, our halls stay alive day and night, and no member stands alone. Loyalty given is loyalty returned — for as long as you wear our name.

Article VWhat Is Expected of You

  1. Grow stronger, every day, without excuse.
  2. Lift up those beneath you.
  3. Speak, and be heard, in our halls.
  4. Give respect, and it will return tenfold.
  5. Carry this name as though it were your own blood.
